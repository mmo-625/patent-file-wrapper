The invention is an interactive greeting card featuring an internal electronics module that plays back multiple digital audio files based on specific user interactions. The current claims define a device where audio playback is strictly tied to continuous user input—such as persistent pressure, specific rubbing motions, or back-and-forth movements—ensuring that audio ceases immediately when the interaction stops. Furthermore, the invention incorporates features that allow the speed of the user's motion to dynamically control the playback volume or tempo of the audio files.
