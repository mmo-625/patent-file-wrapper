The invention is an interactive greeting card featuring an internal electronics module that enables audio playback through specific user interactions, such as continuous back-and-forth rubbing motions on designated card areas. Recent amendments refine these interactions by specifying that the speed and volume of the audio playback are dynamically controlled by the velocity of the user’s physical input. This design ensures that audio is tied directly to user activity, pausing immediately when the motion stops and resuming from that point if the interaction is re-initiated.
