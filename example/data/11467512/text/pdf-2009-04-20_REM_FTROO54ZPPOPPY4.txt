In Section | of the Office Action of November 19, 2008 (“the Office Action’),
claims 1-3, 7, 12, 13, 16, 26 and 29 stand rejected under 35 U.S.C. §102(b) as being
anticipated by Dillon (U.S. Patent No. 6,020,892).

Accordingly, it is submitted that the rejection of claims 1-3, 7, 12, 13, 16, 26 and
29 based upon 35 U.S.C. §102(b) has been overcome by the present remarks and

withdrawal thereof is respectfully requested.
