The invention is directed to a ready-to-bake coextruded cookie unit and its manufacturing method, featuring first and second doughs of different colors that cooperatively form a continuous design element extending from the top to the bottom surface. Both doughs incorporate a specific shortening with a defined Solid Fat Index at 70°F and utilize a targeted sugar particle size distribution to ensure structural integrity and appearance. The process involves coextruding the doughs into a continuous rope, cutting it into individual units using specialized slicing mechanisms, and packaging them for consumer use.
