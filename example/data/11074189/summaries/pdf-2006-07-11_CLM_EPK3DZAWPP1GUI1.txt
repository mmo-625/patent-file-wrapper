The invention relates to ready-to-bake coextruded cookie units featuring multiple colored dough portions that cooperatively define a continuous design element from the top surface to the bottom surface. The amendments clarify that both dough portions share specific compositional parameters, including a defined water moisture content, a shortening with a specific Solid Fat Index, and a precise sugar particle size distribution. These specific formulations ensure that the different dough portions possess similar baked expansion properties, allowing the continuous design element to remain intact and well-defined after baking.
