The invention concerns a ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit and associated manufacturing methods featuring multiple differently colored cookie dough portions that cooperatively define a continuous internal design element extending between the top and bottom surfaces. The dough portions are formulated with specific amounts of water, shortening with a defined Solid Fat Index, and a targeted sugar particle size distribution to ensure the doughs share similar baked expansion properties, allowing the design to remain intact during baking. Minor claim edits throughout clarify language regarding sugar retention on mesh screens and water content without altering the core functional features of the coextruded cookie units.
