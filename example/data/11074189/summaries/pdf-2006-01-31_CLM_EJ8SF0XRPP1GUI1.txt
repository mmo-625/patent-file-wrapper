Our invention is directed to ready-to-bake coextruded cookie units and packaged products featuring multiple colored doughs with specific moisture contents and a defined shortening Solid Fat Index. The claims now more precisely detail how these dough portions cooperate to form continuous internal design elements and distinctive perimeter shapes extending between the top and bottom surfaces. Additionally, the corresponding methods specify the steps of combining these ingredients, simultaneously extruding the doughs into a continuous rope, and cutting the units to preserve the desired multi-colored designs.
