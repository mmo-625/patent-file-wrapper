AMENDMENTS TO THE CLAIMS

A detailed listing of all claims that are, or were, in the present
application, irrespective of whether the claim(s) remains under examination in
the application are presented below. The claims are presented in ascending
order and each includes one status identifier. Those claims not cancelled or
withdrawn but amended by the current amendment utilize the following
notations for amendment: deleted matter is shown by strikethrough; and

added matter is shown by underlining.

Listing of Claims

1. (Original) A ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit comprising:

a first cookie dough having a first color; and

a second cookie dough having a second color; .

wherein both the first cookie dough and the second cookie dough
comprise a shortening with a Solid Fat Index between about 22 to about
35 at about 70°F, and

wherein the first cookie dough and the second cookie dough are
coextruded so as to cooperatively define a continuous design element
between a top surface and a bottom surface of the ready-to-bake

extruded cookie unit.

2. (Original) The ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit of claim 1 wherein the

first cookie dough defines a perimeter surface.
3. (Original) The ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit of claim 2 wherein the
second cookie dough is bounded by the perimeter surface of said first cookie

dough.

4. (Original) The ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit of claim 1, wherein the
first cookie dough and the second cookie dough further define a unit height and

12. (Original) The ready-to-bake cookie unit of claim 1 wherein the

13. (Original) The ready-to-bake cookie unit of claim 1, further comprising a
flavored inclusion selected from the group consisting of a fruit, a nut, a candy,

14. (Original) A method for forming an extruded ready-to-bake cookie unit
comprising the steps of:
providing shortening having a Solid Fat Index between about 22 to about
35 at about 70°F; .
combining the shortening with sugar, water and flour to form a
first cookie dough and a second cookie dough;
extruding the first cookie dough and the second cookie dough
simultaneously so as to form a continuous dough rope; and
cutting the continuous dough rope so as to form the ready-to-
bake-cookie unit, the ready-to-bake cookie unit having a continuous
design element extending from a top surface to a bottom surface of the
ready-to-bake cookie unit, the continuous design element defined by the

first cookie dough and the second cookie dough.

15. (Original) The method of claim 14, further comprising:
positioning the ready-to-bake cookie unit on a packaging tray.

16. (Original) The method of claim 14, wherein cutting the dough rope
comprises slicing the dough rope with a cutting member, the cutting member

comprising at least one of an ultrasonic cutter and a wire cutter.
