Amendment to the Claims:

A detailed listing of all claims that are, or were, in the present application,

irrespective of whether the claim(s) remains under examination in the application are

presented below. The claims are presented in ascending order and each includes one

status identifier. Those claims not cancelled or withdrawn but amended by the

current amendment utilize the following notations for amendment: 1. deleted matter

is shown by strikethrough for six or more characters and double brackets for five or

less characters; and 2. added matter is shown by underlining.

Listing of Claims:

2.

(Previously Presented) A ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit comprising:

a first cookie dough having a first color; and

a second cookie dough having a second color;

wherein both the first cookie dough and the second cookie dough
comprise: water in an amount from 4% to 15% by weight,

a shortening with a Solid Fat Index between about 22 to about 35 at
about 70°F and sugar having a sugar particle size distribution with between
about 18% by weight and about 45% by weight of the sugar retained by a 100
mesh screen such that the first cookie dough and the second cookie dough
possess similar baked expansion properties,

wherein the first cookie dough and the second cookie dough are
coextruded so as to cooperatively define a continuous design element between a
top surface and a bottom surface of the ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit and
whereupon baking of the ready-to-bake extruded cookie, the continuous design
element remains defined due to the similar baked expansion properties of the

first cookie dough and the second cookie dough.

(Original) The ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit of claim 1 wherein the first

cookie dough defines a perimeter surface.

14. (Previously Presented) A method for forming an extruded ready-to-bake cookie
unit comprising the steps of:
providing shortening having a Solid Fat Index between about 22 to about
_ 35 at about 70°F;
providing sugar having a sugar particle size distribution with between
about 18% by weight and about 45% by weight of the sugar retained by a 100
mesh screen;
combining the shortening and sugar with water and flour to form a first
cookie dough and a second cookie dough, wherein sufficient water is added
such that the water comprises 4% to 15% by weight of the first cookie dough
and the second cookie dough and wherein the first cookie dough and the second
cookie dough share similar baked expansion properties;
extruding the first cookie dough and the second cookie dough
simultaneously so as to form a continuous dough rope; and
cutting the continuous dough rope so as to form the ready-to-bake-
cookie unit, the ready-to-bake cookie unit having a continuous design element
extending from a top surface to a bottom surface of the ready-to-bake cookie
unit, the continuous design element defined by the first cookie dough and the

second cookie dough.

15. (Original) The method of claim 14, further comprising:

positioning the ready-to-bake cookie unit on a packaging tray.

16. (Original) The method of claim 14, wherein cutting the dough rope comprises
slicing the dough rope with a cutting member, the cutting member comprising at least

one of an ultrasonic cutter and a wire cutter.
