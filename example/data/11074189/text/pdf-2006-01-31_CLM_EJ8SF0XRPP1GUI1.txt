Amendment to the Claims:

A detailed listing of all claims that are, or were, in the present application, irrespective
of whether the claim(s) remains under examination in the application are presented below. The
claims are presented in ascending order and each includes one status identifier. Those claims
not cancelled or withdrawn but amended by the current amendment utilize the following
notations for amendment: 1. deleted matter is shown by strikethrough for six or more
characters and double brackets for five or less characters; and 2. added matter is shown by

underlining.

Listing of Claims:

1. (Currently Amended) A ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit comprising:
a first cookie dough having a first color; and
a second cookie dough having a second color;
wherein both the first cookie dough and the second cookie dough have a

moisture content from about 4% to about 15% by weight;
wherein both the first cookie dough and the second cookie dough comprise a_

shortening with a Solid Fat Index between about 22 to about 35 at about 70°F, and
wherein the first cookie dough and the second cookie dough are coextruded so
as to cooperatively define a continuous design element between a top surface and a

bottom surface of the ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit.

2. (Original) The ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit of claim 1 wherein the first cookie

dough defines a perimeter surface.

3. (Original) The ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit of claim 2 wherein the second cookie

dough is bounded by the perimeter surface of said first cookie dough.

4. (Original) The ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit of claim 1, wherein the first cookie
dough and the second cookie dough further define a unit height and a widest cross-sectional
length and wherein a ratio of unit height to the widest cross-sectional length is within a range

of about 0.12 to about 3.0.

5. (Original) The ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit of claim 4, wherein the ratio of unit

height to the widest cross-sectional length is within a range of about 0.3 to about 0.5.

13. (Original) The ready-to-bake cookie unit of claim 1, further comprising a flavored
inclusion selected from the group consisting of a fruit, a nut, a candy, a spice, a flavorant and
