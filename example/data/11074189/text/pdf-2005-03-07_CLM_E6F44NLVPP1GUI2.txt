Attorney Docket No. 6109US-C1P1

WHAT IS CLAIMED:

l. A ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit comprising:
a first cookie dough having a first color; and
a second cookie dough having a second color;
wherein both the first cookie dough and the second cookie dough comprise a
shortening with a Solid Fat Index between about 22 to about 35 at about 70°F, and
wherein the first cookie dough and the second cookie dough are coextruded so
as to cooperatively define a continuous design element between a top surface and a

bottom surface of the ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit.

2. The ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit of claim 1 wherein the first cookie dough

defines a perimeter surface.

3. The ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit of claim 2 wherein the second cookie dough

is bounded by the perimeter surface of said first cookie dough.

4. The ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit of claim 1, wherein the first cookie dough and
the second cookie dough further define a unit height and a widest cross-sectional length and
wherein a ratio of unit height to the widest cross-sectional length is within a range of about

0.12 to about 3.0.

5. The ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit of claim 4, wherein the ratio of unit height to

the widest cross-sectional length is within a range of about 0.3 to about 0.5.

6. The ready-to-bake extruded cookie unit of claim 4, wherein the first cookie dough and
the second cookie dough define a generally round shape such that the widest cross-section

length comprises a diameter of the generally round shape.
