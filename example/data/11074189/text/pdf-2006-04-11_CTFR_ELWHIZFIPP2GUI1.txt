Claims 1-22 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112, first paragraph, as failing to
comply with the written description requirement. The claim(s) contains subject matter
which was not described in the specification in such a way as to reasonably convey to
one skilled in the relevant art that the inventor(s), at the time the application was filed,

had possession of the claimed invention.
| In the amendment filed 1/31/06, applicant amends claims 1, 14, 20-22 to add the
limitation “ wherein both the first cookie dough and the second cookie dough have a
moisture content from about 4-15%”. This limitation is not supported by the original
disclosure. Page 11 disclose the water content of 4-15%; there is no disclosure that
| the moisture content of the dough is about 4-15%. The specification does not disclose
that the moisture content of the dough comes just from the water content. It is well
known that flour has moisture content, shortening can influence the moisture content of
the dough and other ingredients such as sugar, egg, color etc.. can also influence the
moisture content of the dough. Water content and the moisture content of the dough
are not the same.

Claims 1-22 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 103(a) as being unpatentable over
Rasmussen in view of Dartey et al., Drantch et al, Hay, Jr. et al and Lengerich et al

Rasmussen discloses a cookie dough intermediate having and integral design
element. The dough intermediate is made from two dough mixes having different
identifiable indicia such as different color characteristics or texture characteristics. The
inner and outer cookie doughs form and interior cross sectional shape of dough that is

visible. The dough intermediate is formed by simultaneously extruding two different

the proper moisture content of the dough so that it can be properly prepared with the
equipment used and that it would give the proper texture and taste. This determination -
would have been readily apparent to one skilled in the art through the teaching of the
prior art. The prior art teaches the moisture content that is typically found in cookie

obvious to one skilled in the art to do so when desiring to enhance the appearance of
the product. It would have been obvious to vary the dough proportion depending on the
configuration made, the particular design wanted and the taste, flavor and texture
desired. It would also have been obvious to one skilled in the art to change the shape

additional dough portion to form different design elements. These parameters involves
routine manipulation of dough equipment and would have been within the determination
of one in the art through routine experimentation. There are many variations in making
cookies, including different figures, decoration, dough, desire, shape etc.. All these
