I've filed a patent application for Autonomous snow removal machine.
Based on the current claims, my invention is an autonomous snow removal machine created by converting a standard manual snowblower with battery-powered electric motors and an onboard edge computer module that navigates using pre-stored maps and sensor data. The design incorporates various customizable features such as a smartphone app interface, swivel wheels, single-battery power, protective enclosures, and safety lights or sounds, along with the capability to manually override the system or spray salt. Furthermore, the underlying conversion and drive technology is adaptable for broader applications including farming machines, industrial equipment, lawn mowers, and large-scale clearing of paved areas.
Claim 1 · Original

An autonomous snow removal machine that is self-operating, using sensors and an edge computer module to navigate and clear snow. Elements that comprise this machine include:

a manually controlled snowblower conversion; whereby the machine is created from a regular snowblower by replacing its manual controls with electric motors powered by batteries;

electrically driven functions, whereby key functions like wheel movement, chute control, deflector control, and auger engagement are now powered by electric motors;

edge computer control, whereby an onboard edge computer module receives data from sensors and sends instructions to the electric motors to control the machine's movements;

pre-stored map for localization, whereby the machine utilizes a map of the area, created during snowless conditions, to identify its location and boundaries;

manual mode capability, whereby the machine can be operated manually when required by disengaging the clutch mechanism at the wheels and other electric motors.

Claim 1 · Original

An autonomous snow removal machine formed by converting a manually operated snowblower, the machine comprising:

a snowblower frame supporting:

a gasoline engine configured to drive rotation of a snow auger that ingests snow and discharges snow through a chute; and rear wheels that, in the manually operated snowblower, are free-spinning and pushed by a human operator;

a drive conversion assembly mounted to the snowblower frame and comprising: at least one electric motor mechanically coupled to at least one of the rear wheels through a clutch mechanism manually engageable and disengageable to selectively allow free-rolling manual movement of the machine; an electrically powered actuator configured to engage and disengage a belt drive between the gasoline engine and the snow auger; and an electrically powered actuator configured to rotate the chute, and optionally an electrically powered actuator configured to move a chute deflector;

a power subsystem including at least one rechargeable battery supplying electrical power to the at least one electric motor, the electrically powered actuator or actuators, and an onboard edge computer module;

an onboard sensor suite mounted on the snowblower frame and comprising at least one lidar, one camera and at least one of a GPS receiver, an inertial measurement unit, and a wheel speed sensor, the onboard sensor suite being configured to generate localization and environment data;

the onboard edge computer module configured to: in a training mode executed when there is no snow or when snow depth is low enough that driveway, sidewalk, road, and other boundary edges are visible, receive the localization and environment data from the onboard sensor suite and construct and store in memory a map defining boundaries of one or more snow-clearing zones; and in an autonomous snow-removal mode executed when snow partially or fully obscures the boundary edges, control the at least one electric motor and the electrically powered actuator or actuators based at least on the stored map and current sensor data to: start the gasoline engine, engage the belt drive to rotate the snow auger, drive and steer the rear wheels, and move the chute and deflector, to clear snow from within the one or more snow-clearing zones while avoiding areas outside the boundaries;

wherein, during autonomous snow removal, the gasoline engine provides power for rotating the snow auger and does not provide motive power to the rear wheels, the rear wheels being driven by the at least one electric motor; and

wherein the machine is further operable in a manual mode in which the clutch mechanism is disengaged so that the rear wheels free-roll and the machine can be manually positioned by a user, and localization is performed primarily using the onboard sensor suite and the stored map, with any static sensors or signposts on the property, if present, providing only supplemental safety and localization information.
