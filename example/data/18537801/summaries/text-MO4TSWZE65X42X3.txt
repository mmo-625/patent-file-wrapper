My invention is an autonomous snow removal machine created by converting a traditional manual, gasoline-powered snowblower with a specialized drive conversion assembly, electric actuators, and an onboard sensor and computer system. The machine operates by using its gasoline engine exclusively to power the snow auger while electric motors drive and steer the rear wheels, allowing it to autonomously map boundaries in a training mode and clear snow within those zones during an autonomous mode. Additionally, the conversion technology and control systems are adaptable for use on other equipment such as lawn mowers, industrial machines, and farming vehicles.
