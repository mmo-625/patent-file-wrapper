The invention has been refined to specifically detail an autonomous snow removal machine created by converting a conventional, manually operated gasoline snowblower using a specialized drive conversion assembly, electric actuators, and an onboard edge computer module. The updated claims clearly delineate that the gasoline engine powers the snow auger while electric motors drive the rear wheels, and they introduce a two-mode operational capability featuring a training mode for mapping boundaries during snowless conditions and an autonomous snow-removal mode utilizing sensors and the pre-stored map. Additionally, various dependent claims now specify optional physical additions like front swivel wheels, protective enclosures, warning lights or sounds, a salt-dispensing subsystem, and smartphone app integration for remote control and boundary-teaching.
