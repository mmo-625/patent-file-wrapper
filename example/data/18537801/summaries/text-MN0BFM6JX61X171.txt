My invention is an autonomous snow removal machine created by converting a traditional, manually operated gasoline snowblower using a specialized drive conversion assembly, rechargeable battery power subsystem, and an onboard edge computer module. The system relies on an integrated sensor suite to map boundaries during clear conditions and execute autonomous snow clearing when snow obscures the surroundings, while the gasoline engine exclusively drives the auger and electric motors handle propulsion and steering. Additionally, the machine retains a manual operating mode with a disengageable clutch, and the core conversion technology can also be adapted for use with lawn mowing, agricultural, or industrial machinery.
