In re Application of: Rajeev Thakur Application No.: 18/537,801 Filed: December 13, 2023 First Named Inventor: Rajeev Thakur For: AUTONOMOUS SNOW REMOVAL MACHINE Art Unit: 3671 Examiner: Alfred H. Tsui CLEAN AMENDED CLAIM SET
1. An autonomous snow removal machine formed by converting a manually operated snowblower, the machine comprising:
a snowblower frame supporting: a gasoline engine configured to drive rotation of a snow auger that ingests snow and discharges snow through a chute; and rear wheels that, in the manually operated snowblower, are free-spinning and pushed by a human operator;
a drive conversion assembly mounted to the snowblower frame and comprising: at least one electric motor mechanically coupled to at least one of the rear wheels through a clutch mechanism manually engageable and disengageable to selectively allow free-rolling manual movement of the machine; an electrically powered actuator configured to engage and disengage a belt drive between the gasoline engine and the snow auger; and an electrically powered actuator configured to rotate the chute, and optionally an electrically powered actuator configured to move a chute deflector;
a power subsystem including at least one rechargeable battery supplying electrical power to the at least one electric motor, the electrically powered actuator or actuators, and an onboard edge computer module;
an onboard sensor suite mounted on the snowblower frame and comprising at least one lidar, one camera and at least one of a GPS receiver, an inertial measurement unit, and a wheel speed sensor, the onboard sensor suite being configured to generate localization and environment data;
the onboard edge computer module configured to: in a training mode executed when there is no snow or when snow depth is low enough that driveway, sidewalk, road, and other boundary edges are visible, receive the localization and environment data from the onboard sensor suite and construct and store in memory a map defining boundaries of one or more snow-clearing zones; and in an autonomous snow-removal mode executed when snow partially or fully obscures the boundary edges, control the at least one electric motor and the electrically powered actuator or actuators based at least on the stored map and current sensor data to: start the gasoline engine, engage the belt drive to rotate the snow auger, drive and steer the rear wheels, and move the chute and deflector, to clear snow from within the one or more snow-clearing zones while avoiding areas outside the boundaries;
wherein, during autonomous snow removal, the gasoline engine provides power for rotating the snow auger and does not provide motive power to the rear wheels, the rear wheels being driven by the at least one electric motor; and
wherein the machine is further operable in a manual mode in which the clutch mechanism is disengaged so that the rear wheels free-roll and the machine can be manually positioned by a user, and localization is performed primarily using the onboard sensor suite and the stored map, with any static sensors or signposts on the property, if present, providing only supplemental safety and localization information.
2. The machine of claim 1, wherein the manually operated snowblower is a gasoline-powered single-stage snowblower.
3. The machine of claim 1, further comprising front swivel wheels attached to a front portion of the machine to facilitate turning of the machine.
4. The machine of claim 1, wherein the power subsystem includes a battery that powers the at least one electric motor, the electrically powered actuator or actuators, and the onboard edge computer module.
5. The machine of claim 1, wherein the onboard sensor suite further comprises at least one additional sensor selected from the group consisting of a lidar sensor, an ultrasonic sensor, and combinations thereof.
6. The machine of claim 1, further comprising protective enclosures for at least one of sensors, batteries, the onboard edge computer module, or other electronic components.
7. The machine of claim 1, wherein the manually operated snowblower comprises wheels or tracks and the drive conversion assembly is configured for use with different wheel, track, or drive-mechanism arrangements.
8. The machine of claim 1, wherein the drive conversion assembly and the onboard edge computer module are configured to be applied to a farming machine having a power source for performing a primary work function and electric actuation for movement, steering, or attachment control.
9. The machine of claim 1, wherein the drive conversion assembly and the onboard edge computer module are configured to be applied to an industrial machine having a power source for performing a primary work function and electric actuation for movement, steering, or attachment control.
10. The machine of claim 1, wherein the drive conversion assembly and the onboard edge computer module are configured to be applied to a lawn mowing machine having a power source for performing a primary work function and electric actuation for movement, steering, or attachment control.
11. The machine of claim 1, wherein the one or more snow-clearing zones include at least one driveway, sidewalk, road, parking area, or other paved area.
12. The machine of claim 1, further comprising at least one warning output selected from the group consisting of a light, a flashing light, a sound output, and combinations thereof for pedestrian safety.
13. The machine of claim 1, further comprising a wireless communication interface configured to communicate with a smartphone application, wherein the smartphone application is configured to permit manual control or boundary-teaching input.
14. The machine of claim 1, wherein one or more static sensors are positioned on the property and communicate with the machine only as supplemental safety or localization aids.
15. The machine of claim 1, wherein one or more static signposts are positioned on the property and provide supplemental location information to the machine.
16. The machine of claim 1, further comprising a salt-dispensing subsystem configured to spray salt for melting remaining snow or ice.
