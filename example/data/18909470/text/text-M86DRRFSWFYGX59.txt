## REMARKS
Applicant herewith elects, WITHOUT TRAVERSE, Invention I, corresponding to claims 1-18.
Claims 1-18 remain in this Application.
Claims 19 and 20 have been withdrawn as the result of the Restriction Requirement.
In view of the Examiner's restriction requirement, Applicant retains the right to present claims 19 and 20 in divisional applications.
In response to the Restriction Requirement dated January 15, 2025, Applicant requests re- examination and reconsideration of this Application for patent pursuant to 35 U.S.C. 132.
Election/Restriction The Restriction Requirement states that the application contains Inventions I, II, and III are related as sub combinations disclosed as usable together in a single combination.
The sub combinations are distinct if they do not overlap in scope and are not obvious variants, and if it is shown that at least one sub combination is separately usable.
In the instant case, sub combination I has separate utility such as image classification; sub combination II has separate utility such as automating software navigation; and sub combination III has separate utility such as interface image recognition and the Applicant is required to elect:
I. Claim 1-18, drawn to an agent trained on a plurality of datasets including text, videos, documents, and images, classified in CPC symbol G06V10/82.
II. Claim 19, drawn to an agent configured to interface automation task workflows comprising a sequence of steps, classified in CPC symbol G06N3/02.
III. Claim 20, drawn to an agent trained on labeled interface images, classified in CPC symbol G06N3/08.
Restriction Election Applicant herewith elects, WITHOUT TRAVERSE, Invention I, corresponding to claims 1-18.
