usage trajectories are further interleaved with text descriptions of actions executed on the images

Election/Restriction
The Restriction Requirement states that the application contains Inventions I, II, and III
are related as sub combinations disclosed as usable together in a single combination.
The sub combinations are distinct if they do not overlap in scope and are not obvious
variants, and if it is shown that at least one sub combination is separately usable.
In the instant case, sub combination I has separate utility such as image classification; sub
combination II has separate utility such as automating software navigation; and sub combination
III has separate utility such as interface image recognition and the Applicant is required to elect:

I. Claim 1-18, drawn to an agent trained on a plurality of datasets including text, videos,
documents, and images, classified in CPC symbol G06V10/82.

II. Claim 19, drawn to an agent configured to interface automation task workflows
comprising a sequence of steps, classified in CPC symbol G06N3/02.

III. Claim 20, drawn to an agent trained on labeled interface images, classified in CPC
symbol G06N3/08.
